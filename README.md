**Abstract:**
The rise of digital administrative platforms has created an opportunity to modernize democratic processes within academic institutions. Traditional student council elections heavily rely on manual paper-based voting and physical vote tallying, processes that are highly susceptible to human error, time inefficiencies, and potential tampering. Furthermore, conventional methods lack real-time transparency and often suffer from lower voter turnout due to logistical constraints and long queues. To address these administrative and security challenges, the proposed Student Council Election Tally System provides a secure, efficient, and fully automated digital voting platform. The system integrates robust student authentication via unique institutional IDs, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to strictly separate election administrators from standard voters, and real-time automated vote counting mechanisms to ensure absolute mathematical accuracy. Additionally, the platform implements strict security measures, including duplicate vote prevention using session tracking and comprehensive audit logs to maintain the integrity of the electoral process. The system features an intuitive administrative dashboard that allows election officials to seamlessly manage candidate profiles, monitor voter turnout in real-time, and generate instant, verifiable result reports the moment the election concludes. By digitizing the entire voting lifecycle, the proposed platform minimizes manual administrative effort, significantly reduces result processing time, fosters greater student participation, and delivers a scalable, trustworthy democratic solution suitable for modern educational campuses.
