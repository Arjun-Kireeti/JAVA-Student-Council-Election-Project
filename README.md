**Abstract:**
The rise of digital administrative platforms has created an opportunity to modernize democratic processes within academic institutions[cite: 6]. Traditional student council elections heavily rely on manual paper-based voting and physical vote tallying, processes that are highly susceptible to human error, time inefficiencies, and potential tampering[cite: 6]. Furthermore, conventional methods lack real-time transparency and often suffer from lower voter turnout due to logistical constraints and long queues[cite: 6]. To address these administrative and security challenges, the proposed Student Council Election Tally System provides a secure, efficient, and fully automated digital voting platform[cite: 6]. The system integrates robust student authentication via unique institutional IDs, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to strictly separate election administrators from standard voters, and real-time automated vote counting mechanisms to ensure absolute mathematical accuracy[cite: 6]. Additionally, the platform implements strict security measures, including duplicate vote prevention using session tracking and comprehensive audit logs to maintain the integrity of the electoral process[cite: 6]. The system features an intuitive administrative dashboard that allows election officials to seamlessly manage candidate profiles, monitor voter turnout in real-time, and generate instant, verifiable result reports the moment the election concludes[cite: 6]. By digitizing the entire voting lifecycle, the proposed platform minimizes manual administrative effort, significantly reduces result processing time, fosters greater student participation, and delivers a scalable, trustworthy democratic solution suitable for modern educational campuses[cite: 6].
